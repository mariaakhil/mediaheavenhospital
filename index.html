About Us

Caring for Aluva Since 2016

MediHeaven Hospital, Aluva, was established in 2016 under the leadership of its Managing Director, Dr. Aneseya P. Varghese, with a vision to provide quality, affordable and compassionate healthcare to the community. The hospital carries forward a legacy of healthcare services that began at the same institution under the name KMK Hospital.
