About Us - MediHeaven Hospital, Aluva

Your Health, Our Priority.

MediHeaven Hospital, located in the heart of Aluva, Kerala, is committed to providing quality healthcare with compassion, dedication and integrity.

We believe that healthcare is not just about treating illnesses but also about caring for people. Our approach focuses on combining professional medical expertise, personalised attention and a patient-friendly environment to ensure that every individual receives the care and attention they deserve.

At MediHeaven Hospital, every patient matters. We are committed to building lasting relationships based on trust, transparency and compassionate care.

Carrying forward the legacy of KMK Hospital, we continue to invest in our facilities, our people and the trust of our community, because the patients who walk through our doors deserve nothing less.

Our Departments & Specialities

Comprehensive Care Across Specialities

At MediHeaven Hospital, we bring together a range of medical and surgical specialities to provide comprehensive healthcare for individuals and families. Our focus is on quality treatment, personalised attention and compassionate care at every stage.

General Medicine

Our General Medicine department provides comprehensive care for a wide range of adult medical conditions. We focus on accurate diagnosis, effective treatment and preventive healthcare. From common illnesses to chronic medical conditions, we strive to support patients through every stage of their health journey.

Developmental Pediatrics

The Department of Developmental Pediatrics focuses on the early identification, assessment, diagnosis, and management of developmental delays and disorders in children.
