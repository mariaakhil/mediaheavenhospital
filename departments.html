Our Departments & Specialities

Comprehensive Care Across Specialities

At MediHeaven Hospital, we bring together a range of medical and surgical specialities to provide comprehensive healthcare for individuals and families. Our focus is on quality treatment, personalised attention and compassionate care at every stage.

General Medicine

Our General Medicine department provides comprehensive care for a wide range of adult medical conditions. We focus on accurate diagnosis, effective treatment and preventive healthcare. From common illnesses to chronic medical conditions, we strive to support patients through every stage of their health journey.

Call: 0484-2623890

Anaesthesiology

Our Anaesthesiology department ensures patient comfort and safety before, during and after surgical procedures. Our services include anaesthesia administration, perioperative assessment and pain management. Patient monitoring and safety remain our highest priorities.

Call: 0484-2623890

Neonatology

Our Neonatology services focus on the specialised care of newborn babies, particularly premature and medically vulnerable infants. We emphasise early assessment, close monitoring and appropriate medical support. Our goal is to give every newborn the best possible start in life.

Call: 0484-2623890

Paediatrics

Our Paediatrics department provides healthcare for infants, children and adolescents. From routine health assessments and immunisation guidance to the diagnosis and treatment of childhood illnesses, we focus on supporting healthy growth and development in a child-friendly environment.

Call: 0484-2623890

Gynaecology

Our Gynaecology department offers comprehensive healthcare for women at every stage of life. Services focus on menstrual health, reproductive health, pregnancy-related care and the diagnosis and management of various gynaecological conditions. We are committed to providing sensitive, personalised and compassionate care.

Call: 0484-2623890

Orthopaedics

Our Orthopaedics department focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles and the spine. We provide care for fractures, sports injuries, joint disorders and musculoskeletal problems, with an emphasis on restoring mobility and improving quality of life.

Call: 0484-2623890

Dermatology

Our Dermatology department provides medical care for a wide range of skin, hair and nail conditions. From common skin infections and allergies to chronic dermatological concerns, we focus on accurate diagnosis and appropriate treatment to promote healthy skin.

Call: 0484-2623890

Paediatric Surgery

Our Paediatric Surgery department specialises in the surgical management of conditions affecting infants and children. We focus on careful evaluation, age-appropriate surgical care and attentive postoperative management, ensuring the comfort and well-being of both children and their families.

Call: 0484-2623890

Dental Care

Our Dental department is dedicated to maintaining and improving oral health through preventive, restorative and general dental care. We focus on early diagnosis, treatment of dental problems and patient education to promote healthy smiles and long-term oral hygiene.

Call: 0484-2623890

ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat)

Our ENT department provides diagnosis and treatment for conditions affecting the ears, nose, throat and related structures. From ear infections and hearing concerns to sinus and throat disorders, we aim to deliver effective treatment and improved quality of life.

Call: 0484-2623890

Plastic Surgery

Our Plastic Surgery department focuses on reconstructive and aesthetic surgical care. Services may include the management of injuries, scars, congenital conditions and other concerns requiring reconstructive procedures. We aim to improve both function and appearance through individualised care.

Developmental Pediatrics

The Department of Developmental Pediatrics focuses on the early identification, assessment, diagnosis, and management of developmental delays and disorders in children. It provides comprehensive care for children with concerns related to speech and language, motor skills, learning, behaviour, social interaction, and overall development. The department supports children with conditions such as autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, and other neurodevelopmental difficulties, while guiding parents through early intervention, developmental support, and individualized care plans to help every child reach their fullest potential.
